These pages are for people who use Open MCP Portal's gateways from an AI client or a script. You need to be signed in, and you can use a gateway when:

- it belongs to a team you're on (a **team gateway**), or
- a platform admin granted it to you, to one of your teams or to one of your identity provider's groups (a **platform gateway**).
