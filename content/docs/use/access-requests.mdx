---
title: "Requesting gateway access"
description: "Find Open MCP Portal platform gateways that accept access requests, ask for access for yourself or your team, follow your requests, and review them as a steward."
---

Some platform gateways in Open MCP Portal accept **access requests**: instead of asking a platform admin by email or chat, you ask in the portal, and a platform admin or one of the gateway's named **stewards** approves or denies the request. Team gateways never accept requests: you use those by joining the team.

Go to **Access requests** in the sidebar's Workspace section (`/access-requests`), or choose **Request gateway access** on [My gateways](/docs/use/my-gateways). The page, **Gateway access requests**, has three tabs: **Request access**, **My requests** and **Review requests**.

## Find a gateway and ask

**Request access** lists the platform gateways whose administrators opted in to requests, with their names and descriptions. Listing a gateway doesn't grant access to it, and gateways that don't accept requests aren't shown.

<Screenshot slot="access-requests-discover" />

Choose **Request access** on a gateway. The dialog **Request access to &lt;gateway&gt;** asks for:

| Field | Means |
|---|---|
| **Request for** | **Myself**, or one of your teams as "&lt;team&gt; (whole team)". Only teams you own or administer are offered. A team grant covers all its current and future members and its service accounts, not just you |
| **Reason** | Required, up to 1,000 characters. Your reason is private to you and the current reviewers. Don't enter passwords, API keys or other secrets |

Choose **Submit access request**. Then follow it under **My requests**.

You can't request:

- more than **10** requests pending at once;
- the same access again within **24 hours** of a denial;
- access you already have, or a second request while one for the same gateway and subject is pending.

## Follow your requests

**My requests** lists what you asked for, with each request's status. Open one to see its details, your reason and the decision note, if any. While it's pending you can **Withdraw request**.

<Screenshot slot="access-requests-mine" />

When the request is decided you get **A gateway access request was decided** (`access.decided`). If it was approved, the gateway appears under My gateways, and the request shows when the grant expires (**Permanent** if it doesn't). The request is a record of the decision, not proof of current access: a grant can expire or be revoked later. [Tool scopes](/docs/use/tool-scopes) and [approval rules](/docs/use/approvals) still apply to every call.

## Review requests

Platform admins, and the people a platform admin named as **stewards** of a gateway, review its requests. **Review requests** lists the pending requests you can decide now, with their count. Stewardship is only this: it gives no access to the gateway itself, and no right to edit it, change its grants or name other stewards.

Open a request to see who asked, whether it's for **The requester only** or a whole team ("all current/future members and service accounts"), and the **Private reason**. Then:

| Field | Means |
|---|---|
| **Grant lifetime (days)** | Optional, 1 to 365 days. Leave it empty for permanent access. The grant ends on that date without anyone removing it |
| **Decision note** | Optional, up to 1,000 characters, private to the requester and reviewers. Never enter secrets |

Choose **Approve gateway access** or **Deny access**. Approving creates the grant at once.

<Screenshot slot="access-request-review" />

- You are deciding access to the gateway, not an exact tool call.
- You can never decide your own request, even as a platform admin or steward.
- Both your authority and the requester's are checked again when you decide. If someone else decided first, or the request changed, reload it and review again.

New requests notify the gateway's current reviewers with **Gateway access is waiting for review** (`access.requested`), never the requester. Platform admins also see the number of requests waiting on the admin overview.

## Privacy

Reasons and decision notes are visible only to the requester and the current reviewers. They never appear in notifications, webhooks or the audit log; the audit log records the gateway, the subject, the decision and the expiry. Requests work only in the browser: no key or automation token can make, withdraw or decide one.

Platform admins choose which gateways accept requests and who their stewards are: see [Access requests](/docs/administer/access-requests).
