**Admin › Retention** sets how long records are kept before the retention job deletes them.

<Screenshot slot="admin-retention" />

| Kind | Default | Allowed (days) | Team override |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Call records** | 90 days | 1 to 3,650 | Yes |
| **Recorded call contents** | 14 days | 1 to 90 | Yes |
| **Audit log** | 400 days | 30 to 3,650 | No |
| **Usage rollups** | 400 days | 90 to 3,650 | No |

- A call involving two teams (a gateway of one team, a server of another) is kept for the **shorter** of the two teams' periods; with neither, the platform's.
- Recorded contents go when their own period passes or with their call record, whichever is first.
- Team overrides are set on the team's admin page (**Limits & retention**); teams see what applies under **Team settings › Limits & retention**.
- Shortening a period asks for confirmation: the next run permanently deletes what's older.

## The retention job

One replica at a time runs the job every `RETENTION_INTERVAL` (15 minutes by default). It deletes each kind in batches of `RETENTION_BATCH_SIZE` rows (1,000 by default), at most 100 batches per kind per run: recorded contents, then call records, then audit entries, then usage rollups. Call records are never deleted before the usage rollup has counted them. It also clears expired consent links, old rate-limit windows and expired [MCP task](/docs/server-authors/tasks) handles. It's idempotent, and a kind that fails doesn't stop the others.

Audit entries and call records are deleted through database functions that never delete audit entries younger than 30 days or call records younger than 1 day. When the operator runs the portal with [split database roles](/docs/operate/database-roles), these functions are the only way the serving login can delete them. If you roll back to a binary older than v0.4.0 while the restricted login is in place, its retention runs fail.

The job's log may occasionally show `retention: clean-up failed — Approval is unavailable`: a clean-up step found the access tables locked by another change and gave up rather than wait. The next run recovers.

**Last run** shows when the job last ran, what it deleted per kind ("Nothing deleted" when nothing was due) and any kind that failed. Each run that deleted something writes a `retention.purge` audit entry with counts only.

There are no legal holds or dry runs. Changes are audited as `settings.retention_update` and `team.retention_update`.

## Fixed periods

Some records aren't covered by these settings:

- **Approval requests**: the argument preview is cleared within 24 hours after a request ends; the rest of the request is kept for 90 days. See [Approval rules](/docs/administer/approval-rules#privacy-and-records).
- **Access requests**, with their reasons and notes, are kept until the requester, the gateway or the team is deleted.
- **Notifications**: see [Notifications](/docs/administer/notifications#retention).
