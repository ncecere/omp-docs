**Admin › Publishing** sets the **Default policy** for every team without its own:

- **Approval required** (the default): a platform admin reviews each server, and each changed capability, before it reaches the company catalog. See [Catalog review](/docs/administer/catalog-review).
- **Self-publish**: each team's owners and admins publish servers and approve changed capabilities themselves, without a review.

A **team override** gives one team a different policy, for example letting a trusted platform team self-publish while everyone else needs approval. Set it on the team's admin page (**Admin › Teams** › the team › **Settings** › **Publishing**). Team-private servers never need approval for the team's own gateways, whatever the policy.

## What a change does

Changing the default or an override **never changes any server's state**:

- Servers waiting for review stay waiting until a platform admin reviews them or their team withdraws them. Under a new **Self-publish** policy, their owners and admins can simply publish them.
- Moving back to **Approval required** leaves published servers published.

The policy in force when something happens is the one that applies: a URL change on a published server, for example, sends it back to review only if its team is under **Approval required** at that moment.

Both settings are revisioned: if someone else saved first, you're asked to reload. Changes are audited as `settings.publishing_update` and `team.publishing_override`.
