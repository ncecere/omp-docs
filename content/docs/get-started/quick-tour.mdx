Open MCP Portal's web app is served by the portal itself at its address (its `APP_URL`, for example `https://mcp.example.com`). It follows your system's light or dark setting; you can change it in the account menu.

## Signing in

The sign-in page shows the instance's name and **Sign in with single sign-on**, which takes you to your company's identity provider. Signing in creates your account the first time; it gives you no team and no platform role by itself. If someone invited your email address to a team, you join it when you first sign in.

When an AI client or a tool sent you to the portal, the sign-in page says so: "An app is waiting to connect" or "A tool needs one of your accounts".

## The workspace

The sidebar's **Workspace** section is the same for everyone:

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your teams, and for platform staff a link to the admin portal |
| **Catalog** | The company's published MCP servers and their approved capabilities |
| **My gateways** | Every gateway you can connect to, with its endpoint and ready-made snippets for your AI client |

## Your team's pages

Below the workspace, the team switcher picks one of your teams, and the sidebar lists its pages. Which ones you see depends on your role:

| Page | Who sees it | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | Members | The team at a glance |
| **MCP servers** | Members (editors and above change them) | The servers the team runs: register, test, refresh, publish. See [Registering an MCP server](/docs/teams/register-a-server) |
| **Gateways** | Members (owners and admins change them) | The team's gateways. See [Building gateways](/docs/teams/gateways) |
| **Service accounts** | Members (owners and admins change them) | Team identities for CI and agents. See [Service accounts](/docs/teams/service-accounts) |
| **Usage** | Owners, admins and editors | Calls through the team's gateways, to its servers and by its service accounts |
| **Team settings** | Members (the audit log: editors and above) | Members and invites, the team's audit log, limits and retention, general settings |

Platform admins and auditors can open any team's overview, servers, gateways, service accounts and settings, read-only.

## Your account menu

Your name at the bottom of the sidebar opens your account menu:

- **Profile**: your name, email, platform role, teams, and the groups your identity provider sent at your last sign-in.
- **API keys**: personal keys for scripts and clients that can't sign in. See [API keys](/docs/use/api-keys).
- **Connected apps**: the AI clients you signed in with OAuth, per gateway. See [Connected apps](/docs/use/connected-apps).
- **My connections**: your accounts on other services that tools use as you. See [Connecting accounts](/docs/use/connecting-accounts).
- The colour theme, **Help** (when your installation sets a support link) and **Sign out**.

The docs write these as "Account › API keys" and so on.

## The admin portal

Platform admins and auditors see a **Workspace / Admin** switch at the top of the sidebar. Admin mode has five groups:

| Group | Pages |
|---|---|
| **People** | Users, Teams |
| **Catalog** | MCP servers (review), Publishing, Internal networks |
| **Access** | Gateways (platform gateways), Trusted clients, Auth providers |
| **Records** | Audit log, Calls, Usage |
| **Operations** | Limits, Retention, Payload capture |

Auditors see every page but can't change anything. See [Administer](/docs/administer).

## Finding things

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>) anywhere in the web app to open the command palette: it finds pages by name and by related words, such as "rate limit" or "redaction".
